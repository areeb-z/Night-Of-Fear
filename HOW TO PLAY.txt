NIGHT OF FEAR
A game by Adyan (and Areeb)

Start: double-click "Night of Fear.exe". Keep all the files in this folder together.
The game works offline. No internet needed. Your progress saves on this computer.

Controls
  Mouse ............ look around (click the game to grab the mouse)
  W A S D .......... walk        Shift ..... run
  E ................ use / pick up
  F ................ flashlight   G ......... fire a flare
  M ................ map          J ......... journal
  Esc .............. pause
  F11 or Alt+Enter . full screen

Best with headphones, in the dark.

If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click "More info" and then "Run anyway".
That appears because the game is new and not signed by a big publisher.
